About me

My path from engineering to applied machine learning and research has been shaped by an interest in extracting meaningful information from complex datasets.

Background

I hold a Master of Science in Business Analytics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from the University of Pune. At UIUC's Data Science Research Services, I supported projects spanning finance, education, and the social sciences.

My industry work has involved natural language processing, entity resolution, and data systems built around real-world complexity. I am interested in moving from building these systems toward studying how AI and information technologies influence human decision-making.
